# AI Resume Builder - Project Scope

## Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle to create professional and ATS-friendly resumes. They may have the required skills and projects but often find it difficult to present their information in a clear and professional format.

## Proposed Solution

AI Resume Builder is a web-based application that helps users create structured, professional, and ATS-friendly resumes from their personal, education, skills, projects, and experience details.

The system will also provide AI-assisted content improvement suggestions to make resume descriptions more professional and relevant.

## Target Users

- Students
- Fresh graduates
- Job seekers
- Internship applicants
- Entry-level professionals

## Core Features

1. User profile input
2. Education and skills management
3. Project and experience details
4. Professional resume generation
5. AI-assisted content improvement
6. ATS-friendly resume format
7. Resume preview
8. PDF download
9. Resume quality suggestions

## Expected Outcome

A working resume-building application that helps users create professional resumes and improve their resume content using AI-assisted features.
